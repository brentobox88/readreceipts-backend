=== APPLE DEVELOPER ACCOUNT ===
1. Go to: https://developer.apple.com
2. Click 'Enroll'
3. Choose individual or organization
   - Individual:  USD/year
   - Organization:  USD/year (requires D-U-N-S number)
4. Fill in account details:
   - Legal name (your business name)
   - Tax information
   - Contact information
5. Complete Apple's identity verification
6. Wait 24-48 hours for account activation

⚠️ SAVE YOUR LOGIN CREDENTIALS IN A SECURE PLACE!
