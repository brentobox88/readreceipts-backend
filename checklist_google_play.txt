=== GOOGLE PLAY ACCOUNT ===
1. Go to: https://play.google.com/console
2. Click 'Create account'
3. Pay  USD one-time registration fee
4. Fill in account details:
   - Developer name (your business name)
   - Email address
5. Complete tax information (US requires EIN)
6. Verify your identity (Google may require ID verification)
7. Wait 48-72 hours for account activation

⚠️ SAVE YOUR LOGIN CREDENTIALS IN A SECURE PLACE!
